Two lines to a past self (240 characters): a warning not to send a 2 a.m. "I miss you" text to a number deleted three times already, and the reason — she answers, six more miserable months follow, and the Chicago job turned down for her never comes around again. One specific moment, one sharp chain of cause and effect.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`).
